Chapter 01 – The Van That Nobody Wanted

«Every New Life Begins With Some Memories.»

Genre: Adventure | Friendship | Road Trip | Emotional Drama
Main Characters: Jayanth, Akhil
Featured Vehicle: Mahindra Bolero Plus / XL 9-Seater
Chapter Theme: Dreams, Secrets, Friendship and New Beginnings


1. An Unspoken Dream

The afternoon was moving slowly.

A young man named Jayanth was walking along a busy road, passing several second-hand vehicle shops. His eyes were searching for something specific.

He wasn’t looking for a new car or an expensive vehicle.

He was searching for an old goods van or passenger vehicle that he could transform into something completely different.

For the past several months, Jayanth had been visiting resale vehicle companies, checking online advertisements, and exploring different vehicle markets.

He had a dream.

A dream of purchasing an old vehicle and converting it into a caravan — a small home on wheels.

He wanted to build something that would give him a different experience of life.

But there was one thing he had decided.

He wouldn’t tell anyone about his plan until he found the right vehicle.

Not his friends. Not his relatives. Nobody.

For now, it was his little secret.


2. An Ordinary Meeting

One afternoon, Jayanth happened to meet his friend Akhil near a shopping area.

Akhil: “Orey Jayanth! What’s going on? Long time, man!”

Jayanth: “Nothing much, ra. Same routine. What about you?”

Akhil: “Same old story. Work, home, eating, sleeping. Nothing interesting!”

Jayanth: “Haha! That’s life.”

Akhil: “By the way, did you meet our other friends recently?”

Jayanth: “No, not recently. Everyone is busy with their own things.”

Akhil: “Exactly! Nobody has time nowadays.”

They both laughed and started walking along the street.

Their conversation continued naturally.

They discussed their daily routines, mutual friends, family matters, funny incidents, work, and other random topics.

Almost an hour passed.

Akhil had no idea what Jayanth had been doing during the past few months.

He didn’t know about the vehicle searches.

He didn’t know about the caravan dream.

And most importantly, he had no idea that Jayanth was secretly planning something that could change his life.

To Akhil, it was just another ordinary afternoon with his friend.


3. The Unexpected Sight

After almost an hour of conversation, they stopped near a newly opened car rental shop.

Akhil continued talking about something that had happened recently.

But suddenly, Jayanth’s eyes moved towards the side of the shop.

There was an old vehicle parked beside the building.

It looked abandoned.

The exterior was covered in dust. Its paint had faded, and the body showed signs of age. It was standing away from the shiny rental cars.

Jayanth slowly looked at it again.

Something caught his attention.

It was an old Mahindra Bolero Plus / XL 9-Seater.

For a few seconds, his attention was completely fixed on the vehicle.

His mind started imagining possibilities.

What if it’s available for sale?

What if the engine and chassis are still in good condition?

Could this be the vehicle I’ve been searching for?

He wanted to walk towards it immediately.

But Akhil was standing beside him.

Jayanth quickly controlled his excitement and continued pretending to listen.


4. Akhil Notices Something Strange

Akhil: “Orey! What happened to you?”

Jayanth: “Nothing. Why?”

Akhil: “I’ve been talking for the last five minutes. You’re looking somewhere else!”

Jayanth: “No, ra. I’m listening.”

Akhil: “Then why the hell are you staring at that old creepy junk van?”

Jayanth immediately turned towards Akhil.

Jayanth: “Which van?”

Akhil: “Don’t act innocent! That old vehicle beside the rental shop. What are you seeing in that scrap?”

Jayanth looked at the vehicle for a second and casually replied.

Jayanth: “Nothing special. Just wondering why the car rental people are still keeping that shit near their shop.”

Akhil: “Haha! Maybe they forgot it even exists!”

Jayanth: “Maybe.”

Akhil: “Anyway, leave it. I was telling you about something important.”

Jayanth: “Yeah, tell me.”

Jayanth returned his attention to Akhil.

But inside his mind, the old Bolero was still there.

He didn’t want Akhil to become suspicious.

He didn’t want to explain anything.

And he definitely didn’t want anyone interfering with his plans before he had even started.

So he continued the conversation as if nothing had happened.


5. An Unexpected Phone Call

After spending some more time together, Akhil’s phone started ringing.

He checked the screen.

It was his father.

Akhil: “One second, ra. Dad is calling.”

He answered the call.

Akhil: “Hello, Dad?”

His father asked him to return home.

Akhil: “Okay, Dad. I’ll come now.”

He ended the call.

Akhil: “Jayanth, I need to go. Dad is calling me home.”

Jayanth: “Okay, ra. Go ahead.”

Akhil: “We’ll catch up later.”

Jayanth: “Sure.”

Akhil: “And don’t disappear again!”

Jayanth: “Haha! Okay, bye.”

Akhil walked away without suspecting anything.

He didn’t know that his friend was waiting for this exact moment.

Jayanth watched Akhil disappear into the crowd.

He checked his surroundings.

Then he slowly turned towards the rental shop.

His expression changed.

The casual smile disappeared.

He looked at the old Bolero again.

Jayanth (thinking):

“Finally… let’s see what you’ve got.”


6. The Secret Deal

Jayanth walked into the car rental office.

A middle-aged man was sitting behind a desk, checking some papers.

Jayanth: “Excuse me, sir.”

Owner: “Yes, tell me.”

Jayanth: “Sir, I wanted to ask about that old Bolero parked beside your shop.”

Owner: “Which one? The old white Bolero?”

Jayanth: “Yes, sir. Is it available?”

Owner: “Why? Are you interested in buying it?”

Jayanth: “Yes. I just wanted to know whether you’re planning to sell it.”

The owner looked surprised.

Owner: “Actually, we don’t use that vehicle anymore. It’s been parked there for quite some time.”

Jayanth: “Can I have a look at it?”

Owner: “Of course. Come outside.”

They walked towards the vehicle.

The owner explained its background and the condition as far as he knew.

Jayanth carefully inspected the vehicle.

He looked at the exterior, interior, seats, dashboard, and engine compartment.

It wasn’t perfect.

It needed attention.

But Jayanth wasn’t expecting perfection.

He was looking for potential.

Owner: “It’s an old vehicle, brother. You’ll have to spend some money on repairs.”

Jayanth: “I understand, sir. First, I want to know the price and complete vehicle details.”

They discussed the price, ownership documents, and condition.

After some negotiation, they reached a tentative agreement, subject to proper mechanical inspection and document verification.

Jayanth didn’t reveal everything about his dream.

The owner only knew that a young man was interested in purchasing an old Bolero.

He had no idea what Jayanth was planning to build.

And Akhil?

He was completely unaware that anything had happened.


7. A New Beginning

After completing the necessary checks and purchase formalities, Jayanth arranged a tow truck to transport the Bolero to a nearby automobile garage.

The old vehicle slowly arrived at the workshop.

The garage owner came outside.

Garage Owner: “What have you brought here, brother?”

Jayanth: “A new project, sir.”

Garage Owner: “Looks like a restoration project!”

Jayanth: “Yes, sir. But I have something different in mind.”

Jayanth opened his bag and took out a notebook.

Inside were his rough drawings, vehicle layout ideas, and reference pictures.

He placed the notebook on the table.

Jayanth: “Sir, I want to transform this old Bolero Plus / XL 9-Seater into a caravan.”

The garage owner carefully looked at the drawings.

Garage Owner: “A caravan? You mean a travelling home?”

Jayanth: “Exactly. I want to redesign the interior, create comfortable sleeping arrangements, add storage, lighting, ventilation, and other facilities.”

Garage Owner: “That’s an interesting project. But first, we need to inspect the mechanical condition and understand what modifications are safe and legally permitted.”

Jayanth: “Of course, sir. I want everything planned properly.”

Garage Owner: “Then let’s begin with a complete inspection.”

Jayanth nodded.

The project had officially begun.


8. Meanwhile…

Somewhere else in the city, Akhil had reached home.

He was sitting with his family, discussing the usual things.

His phone received a notification.

It was a message from Jayanth.

Jayanth: “Reached home?”

Akhil: “Yeah, ra. Just reached. What about you?”

Jayanth: “Yeah, all good.”

Akhil: “Okay. We’ll meet tomorrow.”

Jayanth: “Sure.”

Akhil put his phone aside.

He had absolutely no idea what his friend had been doing after they separated.

Meanwhile, inside the garage, Jayanth was standing beside the old Bolero, looking at his drawings.

Akhil thought it was just another ordinary day.

But for Jayanth, it was the first day of something extraordinary.

One friend went home thinking nothing had changed.

The other had secretly started building a new life.


End of Chapter 01

«”Everyone saw an old Bolero that nobody wanted. But Jayanth saw the beginning of a new life.”»
