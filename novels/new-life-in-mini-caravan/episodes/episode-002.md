## 1. The Next Morning

The next morning, Jayanth returned to the garage. The old Bolero was still parked in the same place.

Garage Owner: “Good morning! Ready to continue?”

Jayanth: “Good morning, sir. Yes. I want to understand the vehicle's condition before making any plans.”

Garage Owner: “That is the right approach. We will complete the inspection first and then discuss the work.”

Jayanth opened his notebook and prepared to write down the inspection results.

## 2. A Careful Beginning

The garage owner explained that the vehicle needed a proper professional assessment. Jayanth listened carefully and decided not to rush the project.

Jayanth: “I have many ideas, but first I want to know what is practical.”

Garage Owner: “Good. We will take it one step at a time.”

Jayanth looked at the Bolero and smiled. The dream was still a long way from becoming reality, but he had learned something important: every good project begins with patience and planning.
