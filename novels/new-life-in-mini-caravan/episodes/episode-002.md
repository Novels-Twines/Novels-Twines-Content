# Episode 02 – The Secret Hands Behind the Build

> "A dream doesn't need unlimited money. Sometimes, it just needs the right people."

**Genre:** Adventure | Friendship | Customization | Emotional Drama  
**Main Characters:** Jayanth, Akhil, Garage Owner  
**Vehicle:** Mahindra Bolero Plus / XL 9-Seater  
**Episode Theme:** Limited Budget, Big Dreams, Secret Friendship

---

## 1. A Dream on a Limited Budget

The next morning, Jayanth arrived at the garage early.

The old Bolero was standing inside the workshop. Its condition was far from perfect, but Jayanth was already imagining its new appearance.

He walked around the vehicle, looking at its interior and measuring the available space.

The garage owner approached him.

**Garage Owner:** "Good morning! So, have you decided what we're going to do with this vehicle?"

**Jayanth:** "Good morning, sir. Yes. I have prepared a basic plan."

Jayanth opened his notebook.

**Garage Owner:** "Let's see."

Jayanth explained that he wanted to transform the old nine-seater Bolero into a practical working caravan without spending too much money.

**Garage Owner:** "What is your budget?"

**Jayanth:** "That's the biggest challenge, sir. I don't have an unlimited budget. I want something useful, comfortable, and reliable. Nothing too luxurious."

**Garage Owner:** "Good. Then we need to focus on essentials instead of unnecessary decorations."

**Jayanth:** "Exactly! I want a small travelling home that actually works."

---

## 2. The Caravan Customization Plan

Jayanth showed the garage owner his proposed interior layout.

His main idea was to make the Bolero useful for travelling, sleeping, basic cooking, and working.

### Planned Features

**1. Dual Battery System**

Two large batteries for supporting the caravan's electrical requirements.

The electrical setup would include suitable battery protection, charging arrangements, fuses, and a properly designed power distribution system.

**2. Electric Stove**

A compact electric cooking stove for preparing simple meals during trips.

The garage owner explained that the stove's power requirements would need to be considered before selecting the batteries and inverter.

**3. Large Foldable Bed**

A spacious foldable bed that could be converted into a seating arrangement when not in use.

The design needed to make efficient use of the limited interior space.

**4. Storage Compartments**

Practical storage areas for clothes, food supplies, travelling equipment, tools, and personal belongings.

**5. Exterior and Interior Restoration**

- Exterior body restoration.
- New paint and finishing.
- Interior cleaning and redesign.
- Improved ventilation.
- Comfortable interior lighting.
- Window curtains and privacy arrangements.

**6. Electrical and Working Setup**

A dedicated electrical arrangement for lighting, charging mobile devices, and powering selected equipment.

The owner emphasized that the final electrical capacity and installation must be determined by a qualified professional.

---

## 3. The Budget Discussion

The garage owner studied the drawings carefully.

**Garage Owner:** "Jayanth, everything is possible to plan, but we need to calculate the cost."

**Jayanth:** "I know, sir. That's why I want to keep things simple."

**Garage Owner:** "Two large batteries, an electric stove, a foldable bed, storage, and restoration work. We should prepare separate estimates."

**Jayanth:** "Can we complete the project in stages?"

**Garage Owner:** "That's actually a better idea. First, mechanical restoration. Then the electrical system. Finally, the interior customization."

**Jayanth:** "Perfect, sir. I don't want to spend everything at once."

The garage owner smiled.

**Garage Owner:** "You have a big dream but a small budget."

**Jayanth:** "Haha! That's why I need your help, sir."

Both laughed.

---

## 4. An Unexpected Visitor

Meanwhile, something unexpected was happening.

Akhil was passing through the same area where he and Jayanth had met the previous day.

While walking, he noticed the car rental shop.

Suddenly, he remembered the old Bolero.

**Akhil (thinking):** "That creepy old vehicle... I wonder whether it's still there."

He looked towards the place where it had been parked.

But the vehicle was missing.

Akhil became curious.

He looked around and noticed a tow-truck mark on the ground. Then he asked someone nearby whether they knew where the vehicle had gone.

After making a few enquiries, he discovered that the old Bolero had been sold and transported to a nearby garage.

Akhil was surprised.

**Akhil:** "Wait a minute... Someone actually bought that old junk?"

He decided to visit the garage.

---

## 5. The Secret Discovery

Akhil reached the garage.

He walked inside and started looking around.

Then he saw something familiar.

The old Bolero.

It was standing inside the workshop.

Akhil looked at its registration details and recognized the vehicle from the previous day.

He walked closer.

**Akhil:** "So this is where that old vehicle ended up!"

Just then, he heard someone talking near the garage office.

It was Jayanth.

Akhil stopped.

He could hear Jayanth discussing the interior layout, batteries, foldable bed, and other customization plans with the garage owner.

Akhil slowly understood everything.

**Akhil (thinking):**

*"Wait... Is this Jayanth's vehicle? That's why he kept staring at it yesterday!"*

He finally understood why Jayanth had been looking at that old Bolero while they were talking.

Akhil smiled.

**Akhil (thinking):**

*"So this fellow has been hiding something from me!"*

But instead of immediately confronting Jayanth, he decided to keep quiet.

He had an idea.

---

## 6. A Secret Conversation with the Garage Owner

Jayanth received a phone call and stepped outside the garage to speak privately.

Akhil saw his opportunity.

He walked towards the garage owner.

**Akhil:** "Excuse me, sir."

**Garage Owner:** "Yes, tell me."

**Akhil:** "That Bolero... Is it the vehicle you're customizing?"

**Garage Owner:** "Yes. We're planning to convert it into a caravan."

Akhil smiled.

**Akhil:** "I know the person who brought it here."

**Garage Owner:** "Oh! You know Jayanth?"

**Akhil:** "Yes, sir. He's my friend."

The garage owner nodded.

**Akhil:** "Sir, I want to tell you something. Please don't mention that I came here or discussed this with you."

**Garage Owner:** "Okay. Is there any problem?"

**Akhil:** "No problem at all. Actually, this is his dream project. He has been planning something like this, and I think he's putting a lot of effort into it."

The garage owner listened.

**Akhil:** "I don't want to interfere with his plans. But I want you to take good care of the vehicle. Please don't compromise on the important things just to reduce the cost."

**Garage Owner:** "I understand."

**Akhil:** "He's working with a limited budget. Please guide him properly, explain the expenses clearly, and suggest good alternatives wherever possible."

**Garage Owner:** "Don't worry. We'll take care of the work professionally."

**Akhil:** "And one more thing, sir. Please don't tell him that I visited or spoke with you. Let him enjoy his surprise when everything is ready."

The garage owner smiled.

**Garage Owner:** "Haha! Don't worry. Your secret is safe with me."

Akhil nodded.

He looked towards the Bolero one more time.

**Akhil (quietly):** "Good luck, ra."

Then he quietly walked out.

---

## 7. The Secret Agreement

A few minutes later, Jayanth returned to the office.

**Jayanth:** "Sir, where were we?"

**Garage Owner:** "We were discussing the customization schedule."

**Jayanth:** "Right, sir."

The garage owner looked at Jayanth and smiled.

He now understood that this young man's project meant much more than just modifying an old vehicle.

But he didn't reveal Akhil's visit.

**Garage Owner:** "Don't worry. We'll take proper care of your vehicle. Let's work according to the plan and budget."

**Jayanth:** "Thank you, sir. That's exactly what I need."

Jayanth shook hands with the owner.

He still had no idea that his best friend had secretly visited the garage.

---

## 8. Two Friends, One Secret

That evening, Jayanth received a message.

**Akhil:** "Orey, what are you doing?"

**Jayanth:** "Nothing much. Just some work."

**Akhil:** "Free tomorrow?"

**Jayanth:** "Maybe. Why?"

**Akhil:** "Let's meet. It's been a while."

**Jayanth:** "Okay, ra. We'll meet."

Akhil smiled while looking at his phone.

He knew exactly where Jayanth had spent his day.

But he decided to act normally.

Meanwhile, Jayanth was busy preparing his next customization drawings.

Neither of them revealed what they knew.

One was secretly building his dream.

The other was secretly helping him achieve it.

And the old Bolero?

It was slowly becoming something neither of them had imagined when they first saw it.

---

## End of Episode 02

> "Sometimes, the best friendship isn't about knowing every secret. It's about quietly supporting someone's dream."

**But one question remains: How long will Akhil keep pretending that he knows nothing?**

Jayanth looked at the Bolero and smiled. The dream was still a long way from becoming reality, but he had learned something important: every good project begins with patience and planning.
